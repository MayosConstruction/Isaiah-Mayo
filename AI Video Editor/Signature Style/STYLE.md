# Mayo's Construction: video style

Page: @its_mayos_construction. A father & son construction crew in Hot Springs, AR.
This is a business page: construction first. Family moments only when they tie back to the work.
Voice: funny, self-aware, down to earth. Never salesy or corporate.

## Look
- Colors: Ranch Red `#C4553B`, Cream `#F3EBDD`, Navy `#1E2A3A`. No other colors in graphics.
- Footage: natural, warm, phone-shot. No heavy filters or flashy transitions.
- Vertical 1080x1920. 6-15 seconds, cut so it loops.
- No @handle or Instagram logo in the video. Instagram adds its own.

## Text on screen (the owner's two chosen caption looks; use only these)
1. **Clean sans (default)**: `fonts/InterDisplay-SemiBold.ttf`, weight 600, lowercase as spoken,
   letter-spacing -0.045em, white, ~64px, soft shadow `0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,.45)`, no box.
   Centered in the lower-middle of the safe band (~1150-1300px from the top). A few words at a time.
   Reference: talking-head reel, "sitting on your".
2. **Serif (emphasis / title moments)**: `fonts/CrimsonPro-SemiBold.ttf`, weight 600, sentence case,
   white with a tight dark halo `0 0 2px #000, 0 0 6px rgba(0,0,0,.8), 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,.5)`,
   ~84px, set a little narrow (`transform: scaleX(.92)`). Sits over the chest/middle, left or centered.
   Reference: "Claude video".
Both are free, close matches to the fonts in the reference reels (the originals are app fonts).
- Title box (hook-stacking format): white rounded box, black bold text, upper third.
- Brand graphics only (covers, merch, signs): `Yellowtail.ttf` script and `ArchivoBlack.ttf` caps.
- Safe zone: nothing in the bottom 25% or along the right edge (Instagram's buttons).
  Text normally sits around 400-650px from the top, or ~1240px when the subject is up top.

## What has worked
- `examples/best-performer-milk-contest.mp4`: "Dad vs. 2 year old" series.
  A real relationship on camera, a competition, and Dad losing. Payoff shown first for 1s,
  white title box the whole time, short captions building to the loss.

## Hook formulas to reuse
- Hook stacking: flash-forward to the best moment + title box + a reason to stay, all in second 1.
- "I tried ___ so you don't have to"
- "I wish more people knew ___"
- "You seem happier." "Thank you, I ___."
- "The biggest lesson I learned about ___ (and why it matters)"
- "Just a delusional contractor that thinks people will follow him because ___…"

## What didn't work
- Montages of work with no person reacting. Captions doing all the comedy.
- Big branded end cards. Anything that looks like an ad or obviously AI-written.
