# Sound rules for every reel

## Sound design (baked into the video)
- **Real job sound on** (drills, mallets, saws) at 40-90%. It's the ASMR hook and it sounds authentic.
- **Hook:** bass boom + camera shake on frame 0.
- **Every cut:** short whoosh.
- **Pain points / red flags:** buzzer + shake.
- **Requests / notifications ("while you're here"):** phone notification ding.
- **Good things / checkmarks:** cha-ching.
- **Lists / steps:** key press or click per item; shutter on "post every job".
- **Before the payoff:** rising sweep. **Payoff reveal:** cinematic whoosh + sparkle, then boom on the final line.
- Sounds: `Setup/sound-effects/` (Pixabay) and `Setup/sfx-made/` (boom, ching, shutter, buzzer, sweep, made here).

## Music (added in Instagram, not baked in)
Trending songs can't be downloaded here, and baking a copyrighted song into the file gets reels muted.
Instagram licenses its own music, so the trending sound goes on in the app:
1. Open the reel in Instagram, tap **Audio**, look for the **↗ arrow** (trending) and pick one.
2. Tap **Volume**: **music 60-70%, original audio 30-40%** so the drills and booms still punch.
3. Line the beat drop up with the payoff (the before/after wipe).
4. If the account is a **Business** account, Instagram only shows commercially cleared songs. Switching to a **Creator** account unlocks the full trending library.
