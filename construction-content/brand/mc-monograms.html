Mayo's Construction · MC Monogram

Round 1 · Six directions · Left chest, white on black
01Shared Stem

The M's last stroke is the C's spine. One vertical does two jobs.

02Hanging Frame

A chamfered C holds the M, which hangs from its top bar on pointed feet.

03Seal

An open ring reads as the C. The M is cut from the chords inside it.

04Broken Pen

Old English stems with diagonal heads and lozenge feet. The third stem opens into the C.

05Overlap

A bold M stands in front of a round C, with a clean cut where they cross. Ballcap-monogram energy.

06Western Mc

A slab-serif M with a small angular c tucked into its V, the way old Mc signage was lettered.
