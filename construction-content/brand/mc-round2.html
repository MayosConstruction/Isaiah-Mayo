01Frontier Gothic

Western-gothic letters with spurred feet. The C's spine is the M's last stem, so it's one shape.

02Blackletter Block

A modern, compact blackletter. The C grows out of the M's third stem like a ligature.

03Old English Cut

Old English kept quiet: wedge serifs and a sharp C cut in front of the M with a clean gap.

04Stencil Stem

Crate-stencil lettering. One stem is the end of the M and the back of the C.

05Heritage Crest

A Roman C holds the M inside it, separated by a clean gap. Reads as one crest.

06Workhorse

Heavy, flat-footed blackletter. The C's back merges with the M's stem. Built to survive cheap printing.
