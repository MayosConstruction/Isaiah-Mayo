Round 4 · Hook layout in six styles
4AOld English

Traditional blackletter, the classic tattoo-and-newspaper Old English. The most heritage of the six.

4BHeavy Gothic

A fat, rounded blackletter with attitude. Reads like a streetwear brand.

4CWestern Saloon

Circus-and-saloon wood type with split serifs. The most West Texas of the six.

4DFashion Serif

High-contrast Bodoni. Thick and thin strokes, sharp hairline serifs, a high-end clothing-label look.

4EStencil

Condensed crate stencil. Industrial and blue-collar, and the easiest to print cleanly.

4FModern Blackletter

Tall, condensed blackletter with sharp diamond cuts. Old English, but sleek.
