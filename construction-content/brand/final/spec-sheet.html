Final · Usage and production
CLEAR SPACE

Size

Left chest: 2" wide (1.67" tall). Minimum: 1.5" wide for screen print and heat press, and 2" wide for embroidery. Clear space: keep a quarter of the mark's height empty on every side.

Placement

Wearer's left chest. Center the mark about 3.5–4" to the left of the front center line, with its top about 7–8" below the highest point of the shoulder. Check it on an adult medium before running the order.

Color

White on black is the main version. Black on light colors is the alternate. One solid color only: no gradients, outlines, or shadows.

Screen print and heat press

  • Use the print files.
  • The thinnest lines are about 0.8 mm at 2" wide. Ask for a fine-line screen (about 230 mesh) or DTF transfer.

Embroidery

  • Use the embroidery files. The hairlines and the gap around the C are thickened to about 1.2–1.3 mm so the stitches hold.
  • Ask the digitizer for satin stitch on the thin strokes, with no underlay on the hairlines.
