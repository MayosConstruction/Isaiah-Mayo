# Reel Style: Contractor Skits

Based on 3 inspo reels (@jake_theroofguy, @trever_resultsroofing, @broadside_construction).

## The formula
| | |
|---|---|
| **Length** | 10–16 seconds total, about 7–12 seconds of actual skit |
| **Format** | Vertical 9:16, filmed on a phone |
| **Camera** | One locked-off shot on a tripod or propped up. No camera moves. One cut at most |
| **Acting** | No talking. Just a face or body reaction: smirk, look away, hand over face, phone to ear |
| **Text** | One caption in the upper third, in white, centered, plain sans-serif font with a soft shadow. The setup line is often a quote ("Sorry, we decided to go with another contractor.") |
| **Sound** | Trending audio or music, no voiceover |
| **Setting** | A real job site in the background: pallets of shingles, a torn-off roof, a deck, a ladder, a crew working |

## Three templates
1. **One-liner (inspo 1 and 3).** A caption describing a situation everyone knows ("When a customer wants to talk to 'upper management'") plus one reaction, held the whole time.
2. **Punchline switch (inspo 2).** About 1.2 seconds of setup with a quote caption. Then the video cuts to **black and white** and lines appear one at a time, about 1 second apart ("He did not leave at 3pm" / "He left at 8pm" / "This happens everyday").
3. **Reaction with a reframe (inspo 3).** About 3 seconds wide, then a cut to a tighter shot of the same reaction.

## Mayo's angle
Your edge is **father and son**, and **you talk to one of us**. Ideas:
- "When a customer wants to talk to 'upper management'" → Isaiah hands the phone to Moss (Dad).
- "Dad: 'This'll be a quick repair.'" → B&W: "It was not quick" / "We found rot" / "Every. Single. Time."
- "'Can I speak to the owner?'" → Moss and Isaiah both turn and look at the camera.
- "When the customer says their nephew could do it cheaper" → slow look at the camera.
- "Sorry, we went with the cheaper guy" → 3 months later, the same customer calls 870-617-3507.

## Filming checklist
- Hold the phone vertical, on a tripod or propped on a pallet or truck, at about chest height.
- Leave the top third of the frame clear for the caption, mostly sky or siding.
- Film 20–30 seconds per take so there's room to trim.
- Shoot in daylight, with the sun behind the camera.
- For the punchline switch, film one take. I'll do the B&W cut in editing.

Note: the Instagram logo screen at the end of each inspo is the watermark Instagram adds when you download a reel. It isn't part of the style.
