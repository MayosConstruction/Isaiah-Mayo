# Reel script: How to stay booked as a contractor (about 55 seconds)

## What the two reference reels do (and what to copy)
- **Talking head, one person, close and steady.** Both open with a face and a sentence in second one. No intro.
- **Word-by-word captions:** one to three words on screen at a time, heavy all-caps font, one key word in an accent color (pink in ref 1, yellow in ref 2).
- **Cut every 2-5 seconds:** jump cuts, punch-ins, or a B-roll/screen insert (ref 2 has about 10 cuts in 38 seconds). That's the "pattern interrupt."
- **A number up front ("3") as the open loop,** delivered in order, with a quick ending.
- **Proof on screen:** they show the thing they're talking about (a reel, a website). For you: the job, the phone, the house.

## What makes a contractor stop, stay and share
- **Stop:** a fear they actually have (no work next month), said plainly by a guy in work clothes on a job site.
- **Stay:** a numbered list with one item held back ("the third one is free and nobody does it").
- **Share:** advice specific enough to send to a buddy ("do this one").
- **Save:** steps they can do this week, not "network more."
- **Trust:** no money claims, no "I built an empire." Just what works.

## Five hooks, ranked
1. **"If I lost every customer tomorrow, here's exactly where my next jobs would come from."** Loss aversion plus a concrete promise. A contractor pictures it instantly. **Picked.**
2. "Your next job is already sitting in your phone. Most contractors never call it." Strong curiosity, but vaguer for a cold scroller.
3. "Slammed one week, dead the next? That's not a slow season. That's no system." Names the pain, but sounds a bit like a coach.
4. "The best lead you'll ever get comes from a job you already finished." Great payoff line; better as the ending.
5. "Stop handing out business cards. Here's what actually keeps a contractor booked." Pattern interrupt, but the "stop doing X" setup is overused.

## Final script

Delivery: fast but not rushed, like you're telling a buddy at the supply house. About 165 words.
Captions: **Anton**, white, ALL CAPS, 1-3 words at a time, key words in Mayo's red `#C4553B`.
Placement: lower-middle of the frame (around 1150-1300px down), never under the right-side buttons.
