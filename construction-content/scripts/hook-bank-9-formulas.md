# Hook bank: 9 hook formulas, built for Mayo's Construction

Based on Dara Denney's 9 ad hook formulas. A hook is three things at once in the first frame: what they **read** (on-screen text), **hear** (your first words) and **see** (the shot). Each hook below gives all three.

Fonts: on-screen hook in **Bebas Neue / Anton**, black text in a white box. Captions in **Montserrat ExtraBold**.

---

## 1. Trigger word hook
| Read | Hear | See |
|---|---|---|
| SCREW PLUGS ARE RUINING MY LIFE | "Every. Single. Screw." | You staring at the camera, plug in hand |
| "WHILE YOU'RE HERE" IS RUINING MY LIFE | "Came to fix one door..." | You on a ladder looking tired |
| THIS HOMEOWNER'S DIY JOB IS A NIGHTMARE | "Who did this?" | Close-up of a bad old repair |

## 2. "Why is it important?" hook
| Read | Hear | See |
|---|---|---|
| WHY WE PLUG EVERY SCREW | "Here's why we plug every screw on a deck, and why it matters to you." | Close-up of plugs going in |
| WHY YOUR CONTRACTOR SHOULD PULL A PERMIT | "If your contractor says you don't need a permit, listen to this." | You holding paperwork |
| WHY A CHEAP DECK COSTS MORE | "The cheapest bid is almost never the cheapest deck." | A rotted old deck board |

## 3. "A day without pain" hook (show the after, not the problem)
| Read | Hear | See |
|---|---|---|
| IMAGINE NEVER SANDING YOUR DECK AGAIN | "Imagine never sanding or staining your deck again." | Finished composite deck, morning light |
| WHAT IT FEELS LIKE TO WALK ON A FLOOR THAT DOESN'T SQUEAK | (silence, then) "...nothing." | You walking across a new floor |
| COFFEE ON A DECK YOU DON'T WORRY ABOUT | "This is what Saturday morning should look like." | Coffee cup on a new railing |

## 4. How-to hooks (three versions)
**How to know if**
| Read | Hear | See |
|---|---|---|
| HOW TO KNOW IF YOUR CONTRACTOR IS RIPPING YOU OFF | "I'm a contractor, so I'd know." | You tapping an estimate |
| HOW TO KNOW YOUR FLOORS ARE DONE | "If your floor does this, it's time." | Foot pressing a soft spot |
| HOW TO KNOW YOUR DECK ISN'T SAFE | "Check these 3 things before your next cookout." | Wobbly railing |

**How do I pick**
| Read | Hear | See |
|---|---|---|
| WOOD VS COMPOSITE: HOW TO PICK | "Here's how to pick in 20 seconds." | One board of each in your hands |
| HOW TO PICK A CONTRACTOR | "Ask these 3 questions before you hire anyone." | You at a front door |
| HOW TO PICK A COUNTERTOP | "Granite, quartz or butcher block?" | Countertop samples |

**How to start**
| Read | Hear | See |
|---|---|---|
| HOW I'D START A CONTRACTOR BUSINESS WITH NO EXPERIENCE | "Here's exactly what I'd do." | You climbing out of the truck |
| HOW TO START A DECK PROJECT THE RIGHT WAY | "Step one isn't buying lumber." | Empty backyard |

## 5. "If you..." hook (call out one person)
| Read | Hear | See |
|---|---|---|
| IF YOU LIVE IN HOT SPRINGS, WATCH THIS | "If you live in Hot Springs and your deck looks like this..." | An old gray deck |

| IF YOUR DAD TAUGHT YOU HOW TO WORK, SEND THIS TO HIM | "This one's for the dads." | Your dad on a ladder |

## 6. "This one thing" hook
| Read | Hear | See |
|---|---|---|
| THIS ONE THING MAKES A DECK LAST 20 YEARS | "It's not the boards." | Joist tape being rolled on |
| THE ONE QUESTION TO ASK EVERY CONTRACTOR | "Just ask this one question." | You pointing at the camera |
| ONE THING I'D NEVER DO IN MY OWN HOUSE | "As a contractor, I'd never..." | You shaking your head at a job |

## 7. "I'm sorry" / "You're welcome" hook
| Read | Hear | See |
|---|---|---|
| I'M SORRY TO EVERY HOMEOWNER WHO HIRED THE CHEAPEST GUY | "I'm sorry. Nobody told you this." | Bad repair close-up |
| I'M SORRY DAD | "I'm sorry, Dad. You were right about the level." | Your dad holding a level, smirking |
| YOU'RE WELCOME, HOT SPRINGS | "You're welcome." | Wide reveal of a finished deck |

## 8. "Native" visual hook (looks like a Snapchat/Story, not an ad)
Film it on the phone's front camera, with no fancy edits. Use the Instagram Stories text tool, or a funny filter on your dad.
- Selfie video in the truck at 6 AM: "Day 1 of building a deck with my dad. Let's see if we're still talking by Friday."
- A dog filter on your dad while he explains joist spacing, completely serious.
- Behind the scenes: "What a contractor's lunch break actually looks like."

## 9. Whisper / ASMR audio hook
- Whispering, close to the mic: "Don't tell my dad... but I think I'm better at this than him." Cut to the plug-setting close-up.
- No talking, just loud sounds: the drill, saw, nail gun and plug tap. Text: `TURN YOUR SOUND ON 🔊`
- Whisper: "This is the most satisfying part of the whole job..." Cut to trimming the plugs flush.

## How to test (from the video)
- Don't change one thing at a time. Film **3 to 6 different hooks** for the **same video body**, then post them days apart or as trial Reels.
- Take **big swings**: when a Reel does well, re-cut it with a totally different hook formula.
- Track one number: **3-second hold rate** (Instagram Insights, under the reel, "Skip rate"). The hook with the lowest skip rate wins.

### Ready-to-test set (same body, 5 hooks)
Body: the screw-plug / deck finishing footage you already have.
1. Trigger: `SCREW PLUGS ARE RUINING MY LIFE`
2. Why: `WHY WE PLUG EVERY SCREW`
3. One thing: `THIS ONE THING MAKES A DECK LOOK CUSTOM`
4. Sorry: `I'M SORRY TO EVERYONE WITH VISIBLE DECK SCREWS`
5. Whisper: "Don't tell my dad..."

## SPICY HOOKS (owner wants borderline-offensive, scroll-stopping)
Rules: polarizing hot takes are fine. Never name or show a real competitor, customer or person in a bad light, no slurs, and no claims we can't back up (no fake numbers).

**Calling out contractors**
- SEE THE SCREWS? YOUR CONTRACTOR WAS LAZY.
- YOUR CONTRACTOR IS PROBABLY LYING TO YOU
- 90% OF DECKS IN HOT SPRINGS ARE BUILT WRONG *(only if we can show why)*
- MOST CONTRACTORS WOULD NEVER SHOW YOU THIS
- I'D BE EMBARRASSED TO CHARGE FOR THIS *(over a bad old repair we're fixing)*
- CHEAP CONTRACTORS ARE THE MOST EXPENSIVE ONES

**Calling out homeowners (playful)**
- "WHILE YOU'RE HERE" NOW COSTS EXTRA
- STOP ASKING YOUR CONTRACTOR FOR A DISCOUNT
- YOUR YOUTUBE RESEARCH ISN'T A LICENSE
- YOU DIDN'T SAVE MONEY. YOU HIRED YOUR COUSIN.
- IF YOU DIY'D THIS, WE NEED TO TALK

**Calling out jobs / the 9-5**
- YOUR BOSS IS GETTING RICH OFF YOU
- I QUIT A "SAFE" JOB. BEST DECISION I EVER MADE.
- COLLEGE DIDN'T TEACH ME THIS. MY DAD DID.
- THE TRADES PAY MORE THAN YOUR DEGREE *(say "can pay", don't promise)*
- NOBODY WANTS TO WORK? I DO. WITH MY DAD.

**Dad / family (edgy-funny)**
- MY DAD IS A BETTER CONTRACTOR THAN YOURS
- WORKING WITH YOUR DAD IS A TERRIBLE IDEA (here's why I do it)
- MY DAD DOESN'T BELIEVE IN TAPE MEASURES
