# Series: "Day __ of scaling my construction company to $1,000,000"

## Why it works
- People follow to see if you make it. Every episode ends on an open loop.
- Same opening every time, so the series is recognizable in 1 second.
- Real numbers (when you're comfortable) = proof + drama.

## The recurring format (every episode, 20-35 sec)
1. **Same opening line, same shot:** you in the truck, talking to the camera.
   "Day __ of scaling my construction company to a million dollars."
2. **On-screen tracker** (top of frame, same every episode): `DAY 1 · $0 → $1,000,000`
3. **Today's move**, shown not told (b-roll of you doing it).
4. **Honest result** (it worked / it flopped / still waiting).
5. **Cliffhanger:** tomorrow's move. "Follow so you see if this works."

## DAY 1 SCRIPT (~35 sec)

**0-3s | Truck, talking to the camera.**
> "Day 1 of scaling my construction company to a million dollars."
On screen: `DAY 1 · $0 → $1,000,000`

**3-10s | Still in the truck (the back story, fast).**
> "Quick backstory. I was a cop. Then a banker. Then a cop again. The stress was killing me, so I quit and started building with my dad. Now we're going all in."
On screen: `COP → BANKER → COP → CONTRACTOR`

**10-14s | B-roll: picking up the business cards (box opening, fanning the cards).**
> "Step one. Contractors don't find the work. Realtors do. So I got these..."
On screen: `STEP 1: BUSINESS CARDS ✅`

**14-22s | B-roll: buying donuts, walking into a realtor office, handing them over.**
> "...and a dozen donuts, and I'm walking into every realtor office in Hot Springs. Every house they sell needs repairs before closing. I want to be the guy they call."
On screen: `REALTOR OFFICES VISITED: 1`, `2`, `3` (counting up as you walk in)

**22-28s | Back in the truck, honest.**
> "Most of them looked at me like I was crazy. One lady took three cards. We'll see."
On screen: `RESULT: 1 MAYBE 👀`

**28-35s | Truck, cliffhanger.**
> "Tomorrow I'm hitting the property managers. Follow, because I'm either building a million-dollar company or a really funny series."
On screen: `DAY 2: PROPERTY MANAGERS →`

## Rules that keep the series alive
- Post **every day** for the first 2 weeks, even short ones.
- **Say the day number in the first second** and keep the tracker in the same spot.
- **Be honest about flops.** Failures get more comments than wins.
- Reply to comments with video ("Someone asked how I price decks...") = free episode ideas.
- Only share revenue numbers you're comfortable with. If you share them, keep them real.
