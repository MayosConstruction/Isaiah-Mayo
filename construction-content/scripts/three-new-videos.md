# Three new videos: scripts

**Fonts for all three** (from your font pack):
- **Hook on screen:** Bebas Neue or Anton (the Impact match). White text in a black box, or black text in a white box.
- **Captions while you talk:** Montserrat ExtraBold or Poppins Bold.
- **Numbered steps / tips:** Inter Tight Bold (the Helvetica match), with one word in Bodoni Moda Italic (the Ivy Presto match) for the "educational" look.

**Filming:** talk straight to the camera, phone at eye level, in the truck or on a job site. Each line below is one breath, and you cut between lines. Power words from your list are in **bold**.

## Video 1: "How to know if your contractor is ripping you off" (35-45 sec)
This one can become a series: "How to know when to replace your floors", "...your countertops", "...your deck".

**On-screen hook (0-2s):** `IS YOUR CONTRACTOR RIPPING YOU OFF?`
**B-roll under the hook:** you tapping a clipboard or estimate, looking unimpressed.

**Say:**
1. "Here's how to know if your contractor is ripping you off. I'm a contractor, so I'd know."
2. "**Number one.** There's no contract, just a handshake. If it's not in writing, it didn't happen." *(Owner collects 50% up front, so never use "half up front" as a red flag.)*
3. "**Number two.** The estimate is one line. 'Kitchen remodel, eight thousand dollars.' A **reliable** contractor breaks it down: materials, labor, what's included, what's not."
4. "**Number three.** They can't show you a license or insurance. If they get hurt on your property and they're not insured, guess who's paying."
5. "**Number four.** Every week there's a new 'surprise.' Some surprises are real. But if the price keeps climbing and nothing's in writing, that's not a surprise, that's the plan."
6. "**Number five.** They pressure you. 'This price is only good today.' A good contractor doesn't need to rush you."
7. "Save this before you hire anybody. And if you're in Hot Springs, you know who to call."

**On-screen steps (one per point):** `1. NO CONTRACT` / `2. ONE-LINE ESTIMATE` / `3. NO LICENSE OR INSURANCE` / `4. ENDLESS "SURPRISES"` / `5. "TODAY ONLY" PRESSURE`
**Caption:** Save this before you hire anybody 🔨 Which one have you seen? 👇
**Hashtags:** #contractor #homeimprovement #homeowner #hotspringsarkansas #renovation

**Series version, "How to know it's time to replace your floors":**
Hook `HOW TO KNOW YOUR FLOORS ARE DONE`, then: soft or bouncy spots, gaps you can fit a coin in, cupping or warping near the kitchen or bathroom, squeaks that keep getting worse, or water damage under the finish. Close: "If you've got two or more of these, it's time. Comment FLOORS and I'll tell you what it'd take."

## Video 2: "How to start a 6-figure contractor business with no experience" (45-60 sec)
Tell it as your own story ("here's exactly what I did"). It's more believable than advice, and it's harder to argue with.

**On-screen hook (0-2s):** `HOW I'D START A CONTRACTOR BUSINESS WITH NO EXPERIENCE`
**B-roll under the hook:** you climbing out of the truck, tool belt on.

**Say:**
1. "If I had to start a contracting business from zero, with no experience, here's exactly what I'd do."
2. "**Step one: make it official.** File an LLC with your state. In Arkansas you do it online with the Secretary of State, and it's cheap."
3. "**Step two: get licensed.** In Arkansas, most home improvement jobs over two thousand dollars need a license from the Contractors Licensing Board. Look up your state's rules, and don't skip this."
4. "**Step three: get insured.** General liability at minimum. It protects you, and it's the first thing smart homeowners ask about."
5. "**Step four: find subcontractors.** You don't have to know every trade. Find a **reliable** electrician, plumber and drywall guy, and learn from every job they do."
6. "**Step five: knock doors.** Seriously. Door hangers, postcards, talking to neighbors next to every job you finish. That's how I got my first clients."
7. "**Step six: post every single job on social media.** Before and afters, the mess, the wins. People hire who they trust, and they trust who they see."
8. "That's the **proven** way. No experience needed, just the guts to start. Follow for part two."

**On-screen steps:** `1. FILE AN LLC` / `2. GET LICENSED` / `3. GET INSURED` / `4. FIND SUBS` / `5. KNOCK DOORS` / `6. POST EVERY JOB`
**Caption:** No experience. No connections. Just the steps 👊 Save this if you're thinking about starting.
**Hashtags:** #entrepreneur #contractor #smallbusiness #startabusiness #hotspringsarkansas

> Check before posting: Arkansas LLC filing fees and the $2,000 license threshold change from time to time. Double-check both on the Secretary of State and Contractors Licensing Board sites, or just say "check your state" without the numbers.

## Video 3: Trigger-word hook, "X is ruining my life" (15-25 sec)
Funny, short and relatable. Pick one, or film several and post one a week.

**Hook options (big Bebas Neue, on screen from the first frame):**
1. `SCREW PLUGS ARE RUINING MY LIFE` ← best fit, you already have the footage.
2. `HOMEOWNERS' YOUTUBE RESEARCH IS RUINING MY LIFE`
3. `"WHILE YOU'RE HERE" IS RUINING MY LIFE`
4. `MY DAD'S TAPE MEASURE IS RUINING MY LIFE`
5. `HOT SPRINGS HUMIDITY IS RUINING MY LIFE`

**Script for #1 (screw plugs):**
- 0-2s: you staring dead into the camera, plug in hand. Text: `SCREW PLUGS ARE RUINING MY LIFE`
- Say, deadpan: "Every single screw. Every single one gets a plug."
- Cut: close-up of you tapping a plug in. "Glue. Plug. Tap. Trim. Sand."
- Cut: wide shot of the whole deck. "Four hundred and twelve of them."
- Cut: your dad walking by. Text: `my dad: "you missed one"`
- End: you slowly looking at the camera. "...but it looks **jaw-dropping**, so."

**Script for #3 ("while you're here"):**
- Text: `"WHILE YOU'RE HERE" IS RUINING MY LIFE`
- "Came to fix one door. One." Cut: "While you're here, could you look at the sink?" Cut: "While you're here..." Cut: you on a ladder, in a crawl space, under a cabinet. End text: `day 3 of fixing one door`.

**Caption:** Contractors, which one's ruining YOUR life? 😂👇
**Hashtags:** #contractorlife #construction #constructionhumor #tradesman #hotspringsarkansas

## Power words that fit construction (from your list)
**Trust:** proven, guaranteed, reliable, fool-proof, factual
**Fear:** ripping you off, burning out, sabotage, threat, failure, panic
**Surprise:** jaw-dropping, mind-blowing, remarkable, little-known
**Disgust (great for "worst job I've seen"):** nasty, junk, ridiculous, crappy
**Anticipation:** little-known, discover, forgotten, mystery

Hook formula: **[power word] + [construction thing] + [you/your]**, e.g. "The **little-known** reason your deck is rotting", "**Nasty** surprise I found under this floor", "The **proven** way to stop getting ripped off".
