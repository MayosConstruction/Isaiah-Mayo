## 4. Fonts: use ONLY these (my rule)

| Video type | Fonts |
|---|---|
| Hooks & titles | Anton, Bebas Neue, Impact, Balboa, Vanguard |
| Talking head | Anton, Switzer, Tempting, Shadows Into Light |
| Daily vlogs | Montserrat, Poppins, Clash Display, Tahoma |
| Storytelling | Playfair Display, Inter, Coolvetica |
| Educational | Helvetica, IvyPresto, Sequel |
| Cinematic | Pinyon Script, Archivo, Futura, Trajan |
| **NEVER** | Courier, Arial, Lucida, Agency FB |

Defaults for my page: **Anton** (all caps) for hooks/titles/punchlines, **Montserrat Bold** for
captions and lists. White text, soft dark shadow, no boxes unless it's the "hook stacking" white
title box. Never mix more than two fonts in one video.

## 5. On-screen rules

- **Safe zone:** nothing in the bottom ~25% or along the right edge (Instagram's buttons cover it).
  Captions usually sit ~250-650px from the top of a 1920px frame, or ~1240px if the subject is up top.
- **First frame is the hook:** a clear, sharp, interesting frame with text already on screen. Never
  a blurry or black first frame.
- **Do NOT add my @handle or an Instagram logo/end card.** Instagram adds its own.
- **No big branded end cards.** They make it look like an ad.
- Keep my **real job-site audio**. I add trending music in the Instagram app.

## 6. What worked and what didn't

**Worked (my favorites):**
- **"Dad vs. 2 year old: milk chugging contest."** A real relationship on camera, a competition,
  and Dad losing. Payoff shown first for 1 second, white title box the whole time, short captions
  building to "I did not win." Caption: "he's been undefeated since birth."
- The hook-stacking format (flash-forward to the best moment + title box + reason to stay, all in
  the first second).
- The "Frontier Gothic" / hook-layout MC monogram process, and the Fashion Serif MC I approved
  (later redrawn in the brand script).

**Didn't work (avoid):**
- Montages of work with nobody reacting; captions doing all the comedy.
- Anything that looks like an ad or obviously AI-made.
- Reusing the **insulation-carrying clip**. I asked not to use it anymore.
- Generic fonts (I gave the list above for a reason).
- Overly specific "kickball night" ministry copy (separate project, see section 10).

## 7. Hook formulas to use

- Hook stacking: flash-forward + white title box + a reason to stay, in second 1.
- "I tried ___ so you don't have to."
- "I wish more people knew ___."
- "You seem happier." "Thank you, I ___."
- "The biggest lesson I learned about ___ (and why it matters)."
- "Just a delusional contractor that thinks people will follow him because ___…"
- "Dad vs. 2 year old: ___" (series)
- "Things that weren't on the estimate:" (checklist list format)
