Text Messaging Terms

Last updated October 19, 2026

Program: Mayo's Construction customer job updates.

What you'll get: texts about your project, such as schedule reminders, arrival times, job updates, estimates and agreements to sign, and payment reminders. We don't send marketing texts.

How you sign up: by giving us your mobile number when you request an estimate or hire us, and agreeing to receive texts about your job.

How often: it varies with your project, usually a few messages per job.

Cost: message and data rates may apply.

To stop: reply STOP at any time. You'll get one message confirming you're unsubscribed.

For help: reply HELP, or call 870-617-3507.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. See our Privacy Policy.
