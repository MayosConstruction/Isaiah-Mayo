Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

What we collect

When you request an estimate or hire Mayo's Construction, we collect your name, phone number, email, and project address, along with estimates, agreements, payments, and messages related to your job.

How we use it

We use your information only to give estimates, schedule and do the work, send agreements for signature, take payment, and contact you about your job.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number, we may text you about your project: appointment and schedule reminders, arrival times, job updates, estimates and agreements to sign, and payment reminders. Message frequency varies by project. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts at any time, or HELP for help. You can also call us at 870-617-3507.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or text-messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Information may be shared only with service providers who help us deliver messages, and only to deliver them.

Keeping it safe

Your information is stored in a private, password-protected system that only the owners of Mayo's Construction can access.

Contact

Questions or requests to update or delete your information: call or text 870-617-3507.
